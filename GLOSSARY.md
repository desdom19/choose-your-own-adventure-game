# Choose Your Own Adventure

A console adventure in which a player creates a character, picks a path at a crossroads, and may face a dragon.

## Language

### Places and encounters

**Crossroads**:
The point in the mountains where the player chooses to go north, go south, or exit.
_Avoid_: Path menu, fork

**Merchant**:
A trader met on the south path who sells Armor and Magical Weapons for Gold.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, vendor, trader

### Character

**Gold**:
The player's money, rolled when the character is created and spent at the Merchant.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, currency

**Armor**:
Protective gear made for one occupation and sold by the Merchant. A character starts without any.
_Avoid_: Shield, gear

**Protection**:
The amount of damage Armor absorbs from each hit the player takes. A character without Armor has a Protection of 0.
_Avoid_: Defense, armor class, AC

**Magical Weapon**:
A more powerful weapon made for one occupation, sold by the Merchant to replace that occupation's starting weapon.
_Avoid_: Upgraded weapon, enchanted weapon
