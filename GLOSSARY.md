# Choose Your Own Adventure

A console adventure in which a player creates a character, picks a path at a crossroads, and may face a Monster.

## Language

### Game

**Difficulty**:
The player's choice of how strong the Monsters are: Easy, Normal or Hard. It's made once at the start of an adventure and can't be changed. Normal is the game's standard balance.
_Avoid_: Mode, level, setting

### Places and encounters

**Crossroads**:
The point in the mountains where the player chooses to go north, go south, go west, or exit.
_Avoid_: Path menu, fork

**Monster**:
A creature the player meets at the end of a path and fights in a Final Fight. The Dragon and the Goblin are Monsters.
_Avoid_: Enemy, boss, creature

**Final Fight**:
The fight with a Monster that ends the adventure, whether the player wins, loses or retreats.
_Avoid_: Boss fight, battle

**Round**:
One cycle of a Final Fight: the player's Strike, then the Monster's Strike, then the player's choice to attack again or retreat.
_Avoid_: Turn, sequence

**Strike**:
One side's attack on the other in a Final Fight: an attack roll, then a defense roll, then damage reduced by Protection.
_Avoid_: Attack sequence, hit

**Dragon**:
The Monster met on the north path.

**Goblin**:
The Monster met on the west path.

**Merchant**:
A trader met on the south path who sells Armor and Magical Weapons for Gold.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, vendor, trader

### Character

**Gold**:
The player's money, rolled when the character is created and spent at the Merchant.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, currency

**Armor**:
Protective gear made for one occupation and sold by the Merchant. A character starts without any.
_Avoid_: Shield, gear

**Protection**:
The amount of damage Armor absorbs from each hit the player takes. A character without Armor has a Protection of 0.
_Avoid_: Defense, armor class, AC

**Magical Weapon**:
A more powerful weapon made for one occupation, sold by the Merchant to replace that occupation's starting weapon.
_Avoid_: Upgraded weapon, enchanted weapon
